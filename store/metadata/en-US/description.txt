How much do you actually pay for subscriptions? AbonnementenBeheren shows you at a glance.

Search for a service, pick your plan and the price is already there. In a few taps you have all your subscriptions together, with your total per month and per year.

HOW IT WORKS
• Choose from more than 200 services: streaming, music, gyms, software, phone and internet, newspapers and more
• Every plan is listed with its price
• Service not listed? Add it yourself with a name, price and period
• Weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly: everything is converted automatically

A CLEAR OVERVIEW
• Your total per month and per year, always up to date
• See which subscriptions cost the most
• Sort your list, edit or remove subscriptions
• Export your overview to Excel (CSV)

ON THE WEB AND IN THE APP
Your account also works on abonnementenbeheren.com. What you add in the app shows up on the website right away, and the other way around.

START FOR FREE
Track up to 5 subscriptions for free. Premium lets you track as many as you like. Premium is a monthly or yearly subscription that renews automatically until you cancel. You can cancel anytime in your App Store settings.

Available in English, Dutch, German, French and Spanish. Prices of the services are those in the Netherlands.
